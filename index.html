Hybrid Production

Real photography. Digital environments.

For campaigns where casting, performance, and print quality are non-negotiable, studio-photographed hero elements are integrated into art-directed AI environments.

This approach combines the control and realism of photography with the flexibility of digitally constructed worlds.

View Process →

Every project starts with a conversation about the brand and the brief.

Each decision - environment, mood, composition - is shaped by twenty years of shooting campaigns for agencies and brands. Images are generated using AI, then finished by hand: colour grading, retouching, and careful compositing.

The same process as any traditional production, without the shoot.

I'm Gavin Goodman - a Cape Town-based photographer, visual artist, and Hasselblad Master.

For over twenty years I've worked on campaigns with agencies including Ogilvy, McCann, TBWA and BBDO, and brands including Mercedes, Adidas, Microsoft and Heineken.

ART.DIRECTED. is a continuation of that work - the same creative standards and level of craft, without the traditional production model.

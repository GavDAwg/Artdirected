Hybrid Production

Real photography. Digital environments.

For campaigns where casting, performance, and print quality are non-negotiable, studio-photographed hero elements are integrated into art-directed AI environments.

This approach combines the control and realism of photography with the flexibility of digitally constructed worlds.

View Process →

Every project begins with a conversation, not a prompt.

You supply a product image.

We develop visual directions and present campaign concepts for approval.

Final imagery is executed with AI, using your product image as the reference.

ART.DIRECTED. is a creative studio founded by Gavin Goodman, photographer and visual artist.

A Hasselblad Master with over two decades in advertising, Gavin has worked with leading agencies and global brands including Ogilvy, McCann, TBWA, Mercedes, Adidas, Microsoft and Heineken.

The studio combines creative direction with AI-driven production to create campaign imagery without a traditional photoshoot.

Developed in partnership with Club 10 and Gavin Goodman.

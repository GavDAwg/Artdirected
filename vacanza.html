Concept Campaign

Campaign imagery

Styled studio and location product imagery inspired by the Mediterranean character of the fragrance.

The campaign draws from the scent's citrus profile, incorporating elements such as orange rind, sunlit surfaces, and Mediterranean textures to create a warm, atmospheric setting.

The result is a series of campaign visuals that translate the fragrance's ingredients and mood into a visual environment.
